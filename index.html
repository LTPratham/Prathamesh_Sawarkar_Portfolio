Why rehabilitation tech

Seeing my father's struggle with paralysis firsthand made rehabilitation technology personal to me. It's the reason the AI Rehabilitation Platform and the Rehabilitation Hand exist — not as portfolio pieces, but as an attempt to make recovery a little more supported through exercise tracking, sensor-driven automation and analytics.

These are engineering projects, not medical devices — I don't claim clinical effectiveness or medical validation for either. But the motivation behind them is real, and it's the throughline connecting my interest in AI, embedded systems and human-centred design.
