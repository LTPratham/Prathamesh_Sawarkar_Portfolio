Who I am

I'm a Computer Science engineering student at Lovely Professional University, pursuing Honours in Cyber Security & Blockchain (2025–2029). My work sits across full-stack web development, AI/ML, cybersecurity and embedded hardware — I'm as comfortable shipping a production React frontend as I am wiring an EEG signal pipeline to control a physical device.

Before university, I founded and scaled JEE.Hub, an EdTech platform for JEE aspirants that generated up to ₹1.5 Lakh a month in recurring revenue — I owned product, marketing, operations and strategy end-to-end before shutting it down to focus on my degree. That founder instinct still drives how I approach engineering: build something real, ship it, and own the outcome.

My patent-filed EEG-based home control system reflects where I want to keep building — technology at the intersection of software, AI, cybersecurity, IoT and human impact. Outside of client and personal projects, I compete in CTF competitions with team LUNATICS.

Why rehabilitation tech

Seeing my father's struggle with paralysis firsthand made rehabilitation technology personal to me. It's the reason the AI Rehabilitation Platform and the Rehabilitation Hand exist — not as portfolio pieces, but as an attempt to make recovery a little more supported through exercise tracking, sensor-driven automation and analytics.

These are engineering projects, not medical devices — I don't claim clinical effectiveness or medical validation for either. But the motivation behind them is real, and it's the throughline connecting my interest in AI, embedded systems and human-centred design.
